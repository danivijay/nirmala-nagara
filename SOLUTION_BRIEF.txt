NIRMALA NAGARA - SOLUTION BRIEF
Clean Bengaluru. Smarter Action.
==================================================================


1. THE PROBLEM
------------------------------------------------------------------
Roadside garbage dumps ("black spots") are one of Bengaluru's most
visible civic problems. Citizens see them every day but have no quick,
simple way to report them. Municipal teams, in turn, get complaints
that are vague ("there is garbage near the bus stop"), without photos,
and without any hint of how big the pile is or what kind of waste it
contains.

The result:
  - The wrong vehicle is sent. A handcart arrives at a pile of
    construction debris that needs a tipper truck, so the trip is wasted.
  - There is no way to prioritise. A few plastic wrappers and a
    truck-load of rubble look the same in a complaint queue.
  - There is no closure. Citizens never see whether their report was
    acted on, so they stop reporting.


2. THE SOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------------
Nirmala Nagara is a two-sided web app that turns a citizen's photo into
a dispatch-ready work order for the municipality.

  CITIZEN REPORTING (/report)
    1. Upload a photo of the garbage (or pick a demo scenario).
    2. Classify it: waste category and estimated size.
    3. Choose the Bengaluru locality.
    4. Review the analysis and submit.

  AUTHORITY DASHBOARD (/dashboard)
    - Live counts of Total, Pending and Resolved reports.
    - One card per incident: photo, locality, date, severity,
      size, waste category and the recommended vehicle.
    - "Mark Resolved" closes the loop once the site is cleaned.


3. HOW A REPORT BECOMES A WORK ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------
Every report is turned into three operational fields:

  Size      Severity   Recommended vehicle
  ------    --------   -------------------
  Small     Low        Handcart
  Medium    Medium     Mini pickup
  Large     High       Tipper truck

Waste categories: Mixed Waste, Organic Waste, Construction Debris,
Plastic/Dry Waste, Bulky Waste.

Severity lets ward officers sort the queue. The vehicle recommendation
means the right crew and equipment go out on the first trip. If the
citizen corrects the size or category on the review screen, the
severity, vehicle and notes update immediately.

Note: vehicle recommendations are prototype suggestions, not official
municipal guidelines.


4. WHAT IS BUILT TODAY (MVP)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - React 19 + TypeScript + Vite single-page app, styled with
    Tailwind CSS, deployed on GitHub Pages.
  - Works on phone and desktop browsers; no install or login.
  - Photos are compressed in the browser before saving (a 7 MB phone
    photo is stored as about 110 KB), so reports stay fast and light.
  - Reports are stored in the browser (localStorage). The dashboard
    updates live when a report is filed in another tab.
  - Three illustrated demo scenarios plus three real Bengaluru photos
    (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA) for testing.
  - Classification is done by the citizen today. The screen states
    clearly that no AI vision is used yet.


5. ROADMAP
------------------------------------------------------------------
  Phase 2 - Real backend
    - Shared database and image storage so every officer sees every
      report, on any device.
    - GPS capture and a map view of black spots.

  Phase 3 - AI-assisted triage
    - Vision model detects garbage, suggests category and size, and
      flags photos that are not garbage.
    - Duplicate detection when several citizens report the same spot.

  Phase 4 - Operations
    - Ward-wise assignment and SLA timers.
    - Before/after photo proof when marking a report resolved.
    - Citizen notifications when their report is cleared.
    - Hotspot analytics to plan bins, CCTV and enforcement.


6. IMPACT
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Fewer wasted trips: the first vehicle sent is the right one.
  - Faster response on high-severity sites through clear prioritisation.
  - Visible accountability: every report has a status.
  - Data for planning: where, what and how much waste is being dumped.
